# ConstructlyCo offer and website refresh

The business owner approved this redesign, the defined offer and publication to the live website on 15 November 2026.

- €750 build for Home, Services, Projects, About and Contact pages.
- Copy drafted from supplied business information; client supplies authorised photos and business details.
- Two revision rounds; target first full preview in 10 working days after scope, deposit and inputs are received.
- 50% deposit after scope agreement, balance before launch.
- Website files handed over after full payment; domain held in client name.
- Extra pages, booking, e-commerce, branding, integrations, advertising and ongoing SEO are separate.
- Hosting, domain and email costs are explained separately. Any applicable VAT is confirmed before commitment.
- Optional €49/month hosting and care, with monthly checks and 15 minutes of existing-content updates; no rollover.
